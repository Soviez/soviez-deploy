# Annual Technical Support

Annual Technical Support is **optional** coverage linked to a specific **License** on your account. It includes **technical support** and **product updates** for that License for the prepaid term you purchase.

---

## What you get

| Included | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Technical support | Priority support for issues on the covered License |
| Product updates | Access to future Soviez ERP product updates for that License |
| Multi-year terms | Buy 1–5 years (or as configured); longer terms may qualify for discounts |
| Early renewal | If you renew before coverage ends, the new term **extends from your current end date**, not from today |

---

## What Annual Support does **not** do

Annual Support expiration **does not**:

- Shut down or deactivate your Soviez ERP
- Block login to your ERP
- Delete your business data
- Block backup or restore

Your ERP keeps running. Only covered support and update entitlement end when coverage expires.

---

## New purchases: Annual only

**New customers** can purchase **Annual** Technical Support only. Monthly support is no longer offered for new sales.

If you already have an **existing monthly** subscription, it continues under its current terms until you change or cancel it. Monthly support does **not** include product updates under the new rules.

---

## How to purchase

1. Sign in to the Soviez customer portal.
2. Open **Support** and select the **License** you want to cover.
3. Choose how many years to prepay (1–5 by default).
4. Review the quote — price, discount, and coverage preview dates are calculated on the server.
5. Accept the Support SLA and complete checkout (one-time payment, not a recurring subscription for multi-year prepaid terms).

You can also renew early at any time; unused time is preserved because renewal stacks from your current end date.

---

## License binding

Annual Support is bound to **one License** — the server instance you select at purchase. It does not automatically apply to other Licenses on your account.

---

## Admin or offline purchases

If your organization purchased support through an invoice or offline arrangement, your administrator can grant coverage to your License. You will see the same coverage dates in the portal.
